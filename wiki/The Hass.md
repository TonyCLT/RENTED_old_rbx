### "Keepeth yond mortal spirits, keepeth yond holy wheel spineth."
<div align="right" text-align="center"> <i> - The Hass </i> </div>

</td></tr></table>


- The Hass is an ancient snake-like demon that feeds off of hate and violence

- It's said that anyone who kills for it is given a wish<br>

- The Hass' wishes have rules shown in the quote "Keepeth yond mortal spirits, keepeth yond holy wheel spineth."

- It's at the center of the <b>[🛈](https://github.com/ReRand/RENTED/wiki/Blackwell%20Cult) Blackwell Cult</b><br> as it powers Andrew Blackwell's endevours.<br>

- Blackwell takes advantage of the cult to try to find loopholes in its rules and see how far he can push his luck manipulating the people of the cult to murder for him and lying about The Hass' rules .<br>

- The Hass' name comes from the name of the serpent from the Garden of Eden, nāḥāš/nahash
