### "Keepeth yond mortal spirits, keepeth yond holy wheel spineth."
<div align="right" text-align="center"> <i> - The Hass </i> </div>

</td></tr></table>


#### Descriptions

- The Hass is an ancient snake-like demon that feeds off of hate and violence

- It's said that anyone who kills for it is given a wish<br>


- It's at the center of the <b>[🛈](https://github.com/ReRand/RENTED/wiki/Blackwell%20Cult) Blackwell Cult</b> as it powers Andrew Blackwell's endevours.<br>

- Blackwell takes advantage of the cult to try to find loopholes in The Hass' rules and see how far he can push his luck manipulating the people of the cult to murder for him even lying about The Hass' rules making them believe he can bring their loved ones back from the dead.<br>

- The Hass' name comes from the name of the serpent from the Garden of Eden, nāḥāš/nahash

<br>

#### Quote & Wishes

- The Hass' wishes have rules shown in the quote "Keepeth yond mortal spirits, keepeth yond holy wheel spineth."<br>
- The first part of the quote, "Keepeth yond mortal spirits," refers to the lives of people as "mortal spirits" saying "keep that mortal spirits" meant to say it will not murder, it will not revive, and it will not mess with the mortality of people so no invincibility.<br> 
- The second half of the quote, "keepeth yond holy wheel spineth," refers to a "holy wheel" that's spinning saying "keep that holy wheel spinning." The holy wheel is meant to represent a timeline of set events that The Hass is not able to massively manipulate with its wishes like changing the course of past and future history

<br>

#### Mechanics
- The Hass is an aggressive hunter who when active will come from the <b>[🛈](https://github.com/ReRand/RENTED/wiki/Basement) Basement</b> up the stairs and will slither on the ceilings stalking the player<br>

- The Hass can be stopped at the office by the basement door but once it's out your only way to avoid it is to flash it to stun it momentarily as it hunts you.

<br>

#### Tips
- Keep an eye on it on the cameras and make sure it doesn't get past the door<br>

- If it does get past the door make sure you keep your eyes on it as it can slither away easily out of sight and catch you by surprise
